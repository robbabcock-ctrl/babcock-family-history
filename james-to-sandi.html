Babcock & Blackwell Family Research · September 2026

James to Sandi

Twelve generations of direct ancestry now documented on the family tree — from James Babcock, blacksmith of colonial Westerly, Rhode Island (b. 1612), down through the Rhode Island Babcocks, and from Ann Blackwell, an Irish immigrant to New York (b. 1846), down through the Brand and Schlottach families — converging in Bernadine Brand and Stanley Ross Babcock, the parents of Bob and Sandi today.
