5. "My Mom's side of the family. She had already passed away by then. From left to right: Ken

This group photo names: Ken Krone (Jeanette's husband), Jeanette (Krone) Rawson, Kristy and Jackie
(Jeanette's children from her first marriage, before she was widowed and remarried Ken), Donna
(Paulsen), Adrienne (Robert's wife), Shaun and Dylan (Robert's sons), Robert himself, Marjorie
"Marge"/"Nana" Rawson, John A. Rawson (Cynthia's brother) and his wife Erica Rawson.

Context from Robert's cover email: "I'm still stunned that we had relatives out there so close and
had no idea! I know it was a different time back then, but I'm still really disappointed Louise and
Wayne did what they did. My Nana, her sister was an incredibly sweet person and I hope she didn't
know." -- reflecting on Louise Jarvis's 1940 adoption-out of her daughter Patricia (Lisa Kelly's
mother), and Robert's grandmother Marjorie's own presumed unawareness of it.
