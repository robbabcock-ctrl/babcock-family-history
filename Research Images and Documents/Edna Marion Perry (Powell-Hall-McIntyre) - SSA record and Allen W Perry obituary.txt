=== "Edna Powell" resolved -- via her father Allen W. Perry's own obituary ===
Image saved: Allen W Perry obituary - The Gordon Journal 26 Oct 1933.jpg
The Gordon Journal, Thu 26 Oct 1933, page 8

Full transcription:

"A. W. PERRY

Allen W. Perry was born at South Addison, New York, on March 31, 1863, and passed away at his
home northwest of Gordon on October 23, 1933, at the age of 70 years, 6 months and 23 days.

In 1884 he came to Nebraska and filed on a homestead northwest of Gordon, and proceeded to make a
home in the then new country. On November 26, 1892, he was married to Bertha Stella Keoure [sic --
Keown] and to this happy union 11 children were born, two passing away in infancy. He leaves to
mourn his passing his widow, six sons, Tom, Ed, Charles and Claude of Gordon, William of Hay
Springs and Lester, formerly of Merriman but now living in Oregon; three daughters, Mrs. Marie
Wilson, Mrs. Lulu Eden of Gordon, and Mrs. Edna Powell of Denby, S.D.; one brother, E. H. Perry,
and one sister, Mrs. Ellis Powell, of Gordon; also three brothers in New York; eight grandchildren
and one great-grandchild.

Mr. Perry was one of the pioneers of Western Nebraska and had no small part in making it the
progressive place it now is. He was a kind and devoted husband and parent and a friendly neighbor
and his passing will leave a place that will be hard to fill.

Funeral services are to be held Thursday afternoon with Reverend McFarland officiating, and the
remains will be laid to rest in the Gordon cemetery."

This obituary (for Edna's father, not Edna herself) is what the "U.S., Newspapers.com Obituary
Index" AI extraction mis-surfaced as an "Edna Powell" obituary entry -- it is actually Allen W.
Perry's own obituary, which happens to name his daughter "Mrs. Edna Powell of Denby, S.D." among
the survivors. This confirms: (1) Edna's married name in Oct 1933 was Powell, not yet Hall or
McIntyre; (2) she was living in Denby, South Dakota at that time; (3) a family coincidence -- Allen
Perry's own sister was ALSO a "Mrs. Ellis Powell of Gordon," so the Powell surname appears twice in
one family, once via Edna's own (evidently short-lived, pre-1940) first marriage and once via
Allen's sister's marriage -- these are likely connected/related Powell families in the same small
Gordon, Nebraska community, though no direct link between the two Powell marriages was confirmed
this session.

BONUS: This obituary is also a valuable independent source for the whole Perry family, corroborating
Bertha Stella Keown Perry's own 1940 obituary (already saved) almost exactly -- same 11
children/2 died in infancy, same six living sons and their locations, same three daughters (here
before Edna's later remarriages) -- and adds Allen's own birthplace (South Addison, NY, b. 31 Mar
1863), his 1884 Nebraska homestead, and his siblings (brother E. H. Perry and sister Mrs. Ellis
Powell of Gordon, plus three more brothers still in New York).
