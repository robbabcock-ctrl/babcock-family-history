APPLICATION FOR MEMBERSHIP.
Issued by authority of the National Board of Trustees.
Chicago, Illinois, March 23, 1916.
TO THE BOARD OF MANAGERS OF THE ILLINOIS SOCIETY SONS OF THE AMERICAN REVOLUTION.

I, John Bowen Root, being of the age of twenty-one years and upwards, do hereby apply for membership in this
Society by right of lineal descent in the following line from Samuel Root, who was born in Southington, Conn.,
on the 3rd day of February, 17__, and who died in Cheshire, Mass., on the 8th day of April, 17__, and who assisted
in establishing American Independence.

I was born in Russia, County of Herkimer, State of New York, on the 28th day of August, 1850.

(1) I am the son of Palmer Root, born 1802, died 1863, and his wife Sally Maria Truman, born 1803/1805, married 1826.
(2) grandson of Aaron Root, born 1773, died 1830, and his wife Betsey Green, born 1781, died 1891, married 1800.
(3) great-grandson of Samuel Root, born 1739, died 1798/1799, and his wife Chloe Palmer, born 1739, died 1793/1797.
(4) great-great-grandson of Daniel Root, born 1696, died 1757, and his wife Abigail Cowles, born 1700, died 1748, married 1714.
(5) great-great-great-grandson of John Root, born 1665, died 1710, and his wife Mary Wescott, born 1667, died [?].
(6) great-great-great-great-grandson of Joseph Root, born 1640, died 1711, and his wife Hannah Haynes, born 1650, died 1693.

and he, the said Samuel Root (No. 3), is the ancestor who assisted in establishing American Independence, while
acting in the capacity of Private Soldier.

Nominated and recommended by the undersigned members of the Society:
Signatures: [illegible] / Ashland Salisbury
John Bowen Root, residence: 26 So. Albany Ave., Chicago, Illinois. Occupation: Bookkeeper (Retired).

--- Notes ---
This confirms the same Oliver Babcock Jr. (1722 Westerly RI - 1806 Leyden MA) already documented in Robert's
tree as a Revolutionary War Patriot, and adds a specific unit citation: "1st Lieut., Capt. Barker Wells' 1st
[Middleton?] Co." — consistent with, and possibly more precise than, the "Lieut." rank already recorded. Also
newly documents the intervening generations from Oliver Babcock Jr. down to applicant John Bowen Root via
Deborah Babcock m. Nathaniel Stillman, and their descendant Fanny Stillman m. [Aaron] Root — a collateral
line, not Robert's own direct line, but useful corroboration of Oliver Babcock Jr.'s SAR-documented service.
