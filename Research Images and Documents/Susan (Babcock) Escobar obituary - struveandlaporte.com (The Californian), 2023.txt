Obituary of Susan (Babcock) Escobar
Source: The Californian (Salinas, CA) obituary page, published via thecalifornian.com/obituaries/sca029153;
also hosted by Struve and Laporte Funeral Home (struveandlaporte.com). Found by Sandra "Sandi" (Babcock)
Walsberg and sent to Robert Babcock, 2026-10-03. Full text supplied by Robert (copied from the live page,
which is gated to automated/AI access by a "TollBit" token wall).

=====================================================================

Susan Babcock passed away on September 3, 2023, at 71 years of age after a courageous battle with
Alzheimer's. She spent her final days surrounded by family and love.

Susan was born January 9, 1952, to Robert and Patsy Babcock in Modesto, California. Her family moved to
Gilbert, Arizona until she was 6 years old and then they relocated to Salinas, California where she lived
most of her life.

Susan went to North Salinas High School and was a proud member of the class of 1970. Shortly after
graduating she met and married Tyrone Escobar also from Salinas. Susan enjoyed working at KSBW in Salinas
after high school and would talk often about her experience there. After having her first daughter she
knew she wanted to be a full time mom. She stayed home with both her girls until they started preschool
and then she saw an opportunity to get back to work. She pursued a career as a school bus driver for the
Salinas City School District and really enjoyed driving and being around children. She was a beloved bus
driver with 20+ years of service until her retirement in 2015. After retirement she enjoyed spending time
with her grandchildren and traveling with her brother and cousin all over the United States.

Those that knew Susan would say she was always smiling or laughing. Much like her father, Bob, she had a
unique way to make friends wherever she went. She used to say that she never met a stranger because she
would strike up a conversation with just about anyone. Her positive and happy spirit was contagious and
would spread to all that knew and loved her.

Susan was preceded in death by her father and mother, Robert and Patsy Babcock.

Susan is survived by her two daughters; Natalie (Danny) Givvin of Salinas and Mindy (Brent) Ballard of
Stevensville, Montana; her brother Dave Babcock of Los Osos. She is also survived by her grandchildren;
Rowan Givvin, Bodie and Brinley Ballard.

A Celebration of Life will be held Saturday, October 28, 2023 from 12:00-3:00 pm at The Club at Crazy
Horse Ranch, 475 San Juan Grade Road, Salinas, California, 93906.

Online condolences to www.struveandlaporte.com
