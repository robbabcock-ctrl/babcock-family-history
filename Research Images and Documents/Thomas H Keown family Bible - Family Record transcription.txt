THOMAS H. KEOWN FAMILY BIBLE - FAMILY RECORD PAGES - TRANSCRIPTION
====================================================================

SOURCE
  The family Bible of Thomas Henderson Keown (1846-1933) of Arroyo Grande, San Luis Obispo
  County, California. Photographed 14 Nov 2026 by a living descendant who owns the Bible
  (a great-granddaughter line through Ethel Phoebe Keown Ball; name withheld because this
  repository is public). Photos emailed to Robert Babcock 11-18 Nov 2026 and saved here as the
  13 "Thomas H Keown family Bible - ..." image files in this folder.

PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION
  - Cloth-bound pocket "Holy Bible," brown/grey cloth, "HOLY BIBLE" stamped on cover and spine.
  - Title page: "The Holy Bible, containing the Old and New Testaments ... New York: American
    Bible Society, instituted in the year MDCCCXVI. 1911. [Brevier, 12mo.]"
  - Owner inscription on the front free page: "Thomas H. Keown / Arroyo Grande / California /
    Oct 25 - 1923" (25 Oct 1923 = his own birthday, his 77th by the Bible's own birth date).
  - A "Family Record" section (printed Births / Marriages / Deaths pages) is bound in; the
    record pages are written in several hands and inks (brown-black ink for the original
    entries; later blue and pencil additions through at least 1955).
  - A small piece of newspaper (a fragment reading "...ifornia") is tucked in at the top of the
    record pages - not yet photographed or read.
  - A 48-star U.S. flag is kept pressed in the Bible at 1 Kings ch. 5-6 (p. 355).

CONFIDENCE NOTE
  Readings are from phone photos of old cursive; uncertain letters/digits are marked [?].
  Entries are mostly in one hand and look COPIED into this 1911 Bible no earlier than 1923, so
  they are a compiled family record, not contemporaneous entries. Treat as a strong
  secondary source until matched to vital records.

WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE RESEARCH LOG (see Research Log - Master Notes.md, Keown section)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Thomas's birth: "Oct 25 - 1846, Ohio county Kentucky" in the family's own record. Agrees
     with the carved gravestone (1846) and the 1860 census/obituary birthplace; disagrees
     only with the 1844 in the 1933 newspapers.
  2. Names his parents "Polly Anne & Nathan J. Keown": Polly = Mary Ann (Iler) - the
     1836 Ohio Co KY marriage. Explains the "Mary A Keown" oddity.
  3. Names Phoebe's parents Gideon and Julia Anne McKinney and her birthplace Burlington,
     Des Moines Co., Iowa (born 16 Jul 1850). "(Pingery)" pencilled in is a lead for Julia
     Anne's maiden name - UNVERIFIED.
  4. Gives exact birth dates and places for all six children (Kansas x4, Nebraska x2).
  5. Gives all four daughters' marriage dates and places.
  6. Lists TEN Perry grandchildren (Claude A., Mary E., Lula E., Thomas & John [twins?],
     Edward, Lester J., Edna M., William N., Clara, Charles B.); the log had eight.
  7. Death entries for relatives not previously identified: "Aunt Ella Keown" (d. 5 Jan 1945),
     "Anna Keown Williams" (d. 8 Dec 1952), "Mary Ellen Roby" (d. Aug 1953), and
     "Willie D. Ball" (d. 20 Jul 1955) - relationships NOT stated.
