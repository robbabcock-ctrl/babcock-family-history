RECONSTRUCTED TEXT
"Family nose-counting has become one of the activities of the Kiel-Miller fight for the Republican nomination for Mayor. Miller headquarters last Wednesday made known that 18 members of the Schlapprizzi family, residing in three South Side wards, intended to vote for Mayor Miller. Almost twice as many, and Kiel headquarters came back today with a list of 35 voters, members, by birth or marriage, of the Brand family of 3322 Ohio avenue, who are for Kiel. There are several different surnames in the group, so that the list does not have the regimented look of the Schlapprizzis, who all bear one name. But the Kiel managers cite the fact that none of the Brands or their in-laws is in the employ of the city. Two Schlapprizzis are identified in the city directory as city employes.
The Brand family supporters is: Mr. and Mrs. Jacob Brand, Mrs. Dave Brand and Miss M. Brand, 3322 Ohio avenue; Dave Brand, 3228 Ohio; Mr. and Mrs. Pete Brand, 4910 Michigan avenue; Mr. and Mrs. Phil Staff, Lee Staff and Mr. and Mrs. H. Bohmeyer [?], 3320 Oregon avenue; M. ANALYSIS (agent note)
- Candidate NEW FINDING: a 1929 newspaper that ties the research log's Brand household (Jacob Emil Brand at 3322 Ohio Ave in 1929 per the log's directories; wife Marietta) to the Schlottach in-laws in a single document: "Mr. and Mrs. Jacob Brand, Mrs. Dave Brand and Miss M. Brand, 3322 Ohio" = Jacob + Marietta, plus (probably) their son David's wife Stella ("Mrs. Dave Brand") and daughter Marietta ("Miss M. Brand", b. ~1910); "Dave Brand, 3228 Ohio" = a second Dave address nearby (perhaps David George Brand himself, b. 1905 [UNCONFIRMED], or a cousin; "Pete Brand, 4910 Michigan Ave" is another Brand not in the log - possibly Jacob's step-brother Peter Brand, son of Joseph Brand's second wife [log: stepsiblings William Brandt, Peter Brand, Cathrina Brand] - UNCONFIRMED but a plausible match: same surname, same city, same family bloc).
- "Fred and Lydia Schlottach and Mrs. M. Schlottach" and "Mrs. Clara Schlottach" are listed as Brand in-laws "by birth or marriage". Clara = Clara Jane (Sewell) Schlottach, widow of Robert C. (d. 1922), mother of Stella (Mrs. Dave Brand); Fred and Lydia are two of the eight Schlottach siblings named in Ferd H. Schlottach's 1937 death notice (Ferd H./Fred; Lydia), and Tennessee Ave is where St. Luke's Ev. Church (2336 Tennessee av) stood - consistent with their 1937 funeral at that church. Strong consistency, not proof, that Fred/Lydia/Ferd H. are Robert C.'s brother and sister.
- The Brand clan counted 35 voters "by birth or marriage" - this directly tests the "big Brand family in St. Louis" lore from Bernadine (the family WAS large and cohesive in south St. Louis, Ohio Ave/Oregon Ave/Michigan Ave neighbourhood) even though no "developer" evidence exists (see log 2026-09-01/02).
- Context: the Brand bloc was claimed by the Kiel campaign in the Feb-Apr 1929 Republican mayoral primary (Kiel v. Miller); the list is a campaign claim, not a poll. All people named are 1929 adults.
