RITES SET FOR VIET CASUALTY

Final rites will be held at 2:30 p.m. Monday in People's Funeral Chapel for Jerry Wayne Looney, 21,
Hanford's first casualty in the Viet Nam war. He will be buried in Grangeville cemetery.

Looney died in action June 11 in South Viet Nam, according to the official release from the
Department of Defense. He was a private first class in the Army, had been in the armed forces for
nine months and on Viet Nam duty for three months.

Looney was born in Campbell, Mo., and had lived here [Hanford] 16 years prior to joining the Army.
He attended Kings River School and graduated in 1963 from Hanford High School where he was a member
of the track and cross country teams.

Parents of the young serviceman are Mr. and Mrs. Lehman Looney of 11749 Excelsior Ave. He also is
survived by three brothers, Larry Looney, with the U.S. Army at Ft. Ord, Mike and Garry Looney, both
of Hanford, and a sister, Barbara Looney of Hanford.
