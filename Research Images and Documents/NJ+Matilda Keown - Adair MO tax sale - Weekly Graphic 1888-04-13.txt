By virtue and authority of an execution, issued from the office of the clerk of the circuit court of Adair county, Missouri, on the 6th day of January 188[8], made returnable at the April term ... in favor of the State of Missouri, at the relation and to the use of [?] Strock[?], collector of the revenue for Adair Co. Mo., and against MATILDA NICHOLSON, MATILDA KEOWN and N. J. KEOWN, in pursuance of a judgment and order of sale of said circuit court at its January term, I have levied upon and seized, and on Wednesday the [2?]th day of April 188[8], will sell at the court house door in the town of Kirksville, Adair county, Missouri ... the following described real estate ...: West half of lots ... four (4) ... and eight (8) in block no. four (4), [town illegible], Adair county, Missouri, to satisfy said execution and costs. J. H. KINNEAR, Sheriff Adair Co., Mo."
