A. FRESNO BEE, 5 Feb 1923, article "One Dead And Many Hurt In 3 Valley Conflagrations" (ID FHD19230205.2.176)
 "Two Dinuba homes were completely destroyed by fire on Sunday and a third badly damaged. In each instance oil stoves furnished the origin of the flames. The home of O. H. Collins was first to take fire ... Children playing about an oil stove in the Arthur Babcock home overturned, it resulting in total loss of home and contents. An adjoining home was saved by the prompt work of firemen."

B. VISALIA TIMES-DELTA, 10 Feb 1932, p.6 "Damages Asked In Local Suit" (ID VTD19320210.1.6)
 "Mr. and Mrs. Rawleigh Babcock today filed suit in superior court here against Gus Danos and Virginia Newton demanding $373.__ automobile damages as a result of a car collision which occurred in Dinuba last July. Virginia Newton was driving Danos' automobile when it crashed into the Babcock machine at the intersection of College and Tulare avenues in Dinuba. The amount asked includes the car damage and loss of use of the machine."
