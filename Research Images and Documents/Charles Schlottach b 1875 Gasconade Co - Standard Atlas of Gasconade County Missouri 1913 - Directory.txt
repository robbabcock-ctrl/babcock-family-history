ANALYSIS (agent note)
- Charles Schlottach, b. 1875 Gasconade Co., farmer near Bland (Sec. 10, T42N R6W, ~ Gasconade/Maries line). Born 3 years after Robert C. Schlottach (b. 18 Apr 1872 Gasconade Co.): a plausible younger brother (UNCONFIRMED - no parent named). Matches "Chas. G. Schlottach, Owensville" in the Hermanner Volksblatt 22 Nov 1918 draft-registration list (loc.gov/item/sn93060116/1918-11-22/ed-1/).
- The 1913 plat pages themselves (images) would show whether Ferdinand H. Schlottach or Albertine owned land in Gasconade Co. in 1913 (he d. 1916) - worth viewing manually: https://digital.shsmo.org/digital/collection/plat/id/5435
