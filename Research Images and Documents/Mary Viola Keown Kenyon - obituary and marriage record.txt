Mary Viola Keown Kenyon (b. abt 1881, Nebraska - d. 9 Sep 1939, Santa Cruz, California)
Daughter of Thomas Henderson Keown + Phoebe Ellen McKinney; sister of Ethel (Keown) Ball and Edna
(Keown) Schultz. Her Nebraska birthplace matches the family's move to Hay Springs, NE by 1883 (see
Bertha Stella Keown Perry's obituary, saved elsewhere in this project).

=== Obituary ===
Image saved: Mary Viola Keown Kenyon obituary - Santa Cruz Sentinel 12 Sep 1939.jpg
Santa Cruz Sentinel, Tue 12 Sep 1939, page 8

Full transcription:

"MARY V. KENYON

Funeral services for Mary V. Kenyon were held yesterday at the chapel of White's Mortuary and were
conducted by Rev. John F. Wilson of the First Methodist church. Inurnment was at the I.O.O.F.
crematory.

Mrs. Kenyon was 58 years of age and was born in Nebraska. Her home was in Stockton.

She leaves to mourn her passing her husband, Percy Kenyon; two sisters, Mrs. Eda K. Schultz and
Miss Ethel M. Ball of Santa Cruz."

NOTE: This obituary's very brief sibling list ("Miss Ethel M. Ball" -- listed as unmarried "Miss"
despite Ethel actually being twice-married Mrs. Jesse Ball per her own 1966 obituary, and placed
in "Santa Cruz" rather than her actual home of Orcutt) appears to contain minor inaccuracies,
likely because Mary's own obituary was written by someone less familiar with her extended family's
current details than her sisters' own obituaries were. The core facts (husband Percy Kenyon, two
sisters Eda/Edna Schultz and Ethel Ball, Nebraska birth, Stockton CA residence) are all consistent
with and corroborated by the other Keown-family records saved elsewhere in this project.
