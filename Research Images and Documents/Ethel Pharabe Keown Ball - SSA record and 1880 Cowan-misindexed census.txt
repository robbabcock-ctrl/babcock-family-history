=== U.S., Newspapers.com Obituary Index -- full obituary ===
Image saved: Ethel Pharabe (Keown) Ball obituary - Arroyo Grande Valley Herald Recorder 20 Oct 1966.jpg
The Arroyo Grande Valley Herald Recorder, Thu 20 Oct 1966, page 13

Full transcription:

"Mrs. Ethel P. Ball

ORCUTT -- Funeral services for Mrs. Jesse Ball (Ethel Keown) 88, of Orcutt, were held last
Thursday afternoon in the Dudley-Hoffman Chapel in Santa Maria. The Rev. Herman W. Walters of the
First Foursquare Church officiated at the chapel service and also at the final graveside rites in
the Arroyo Grande District cemetery where interment took place in the Ball family plot.

Mrs. Ball died Oct. 10 in a Santa Maria hospital after several months of failing health. She was
born Sept. 3, 1878, in Cloud County, Kansas, and came to Arroyo Grande with her late parents,
Thomas E. and Phoebe Keown, in March, 1896. She lived in Arroyo Grande until 1907 when she and her
late husband moved to the Santa Maria-Orcutt area.

Survivors are a daughter, Mrs. Bob Simmons, (Myrth) of Orcutt; a son, Charles Ball of Gardena; six
grandchildren, 15 great-grandchildren, one great-great-grandchild [text cuts off at column edge]."

This DEFINITIVELY resolves the "location disputed" note on this checklist item: Ethel's death and
obituary are correctly placed in Santa Maria / Orcutt, Santa Barbara County, California (NOT any
other location previously considered across two research sessions). Also newly confirms: her
husband was named Jesse Ball; the family arrived in Arroyo Grande in March 1896 (one year earlier
than the "1897" date given in Thomas & Phoebe's own 61st-anniversary article -- minor discrepancy
between two independent sources, both plausible); she and Jesse moved to Santa Maria-Orcutt in
1907; children Myrth (Mrs. Bob Simmons) and Charles Ball.
