"SUTTANAMAN LAID TO REST . * Arthur Babcock Leaves Widow and Six Children; Buried At Smith Mountain
BUT.TANA, Stay 31.--The funeral of the late Arthur Babcock who passed away last Friday, was held at the Methodist church Sunday afternoon. The body was interred in the Smith Mountain cemetery. The deceased had been suffering from tuberculosis for the past two years and was at Springville at the time of his death. He leaves to mourn his loss a widow and six children."
