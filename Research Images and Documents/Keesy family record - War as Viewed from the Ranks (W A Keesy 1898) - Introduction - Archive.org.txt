TRANSCRIPTION OF THE INTRODUCTION (family section), cleaned from OCR:
"The author of this work is the son of John Keesy, (Geesy,) a pioneer of Richmond township, Huron county, Ohio. Henry Keesy was a resident of Lancaster and Dauphin counties in one of which John was born in 1804. Henry's father was a Revolutionary soldier and it is said that Henry was in the war of 1812. John, (born in 1804,) the son of Henry, was married to Elizabeth Gons, (born in 1808,) in 1828. To them were born:
 Harriet, February 6th, 1829.
 John H., January 10th, 1831.
 Margaret, March 13th, 1833.
 Peter B. F., May 17th, 1835. Died November 15th, 1892.
 Noah Miley, February 5th, 1838.
 Catharine, August 9th, 1840. Died ----.
 William Allen, July 25th, 1843.
 Mary Ann, April 11th, 1846.
 George Washington, June 24th, 1848. Died August 13th, 1896.
 Sarah Elizabeth, March 5th, 1852.
The parents of these children were struggling with the very few Pioneer settlers of Richmond township to brush away the forests ... when in 1843, on the 25th day of July, William A., (the author,) was born. ... On the 18th day of January, 1859, when our subject was but 15 years of age, his father died. At the age of 18 he enlisted in the army and after the war, in 1868, on the 7th day of July, he was married to ... Miss Maggie J. Lane. ... Elizabeth Keesy, the mother of our subject, died in September, 1875 ... After the war Mr. Keesy entered the ministry ... Church of the United Brethren in Christ."
(Later family of the author: children Minerva 1869, Mary 1871, Maggie E. 1873-1878 by Maggie J. Lane; m. 2nd 9 Feb 1875 Hattie Augusta Charles (b. 14 Sep 1856), children Flora 1876, Oce Ola 1878, Vesta Leona 1881, twins 16 Aug 1883, Leon Castle 1888, Fern 1891, Helen 1896, Robert Ivan 1898.)

OTHER FAMILY MENTIONS IN THE MEMOIR (OCR lines noted)

- l.3600ff (ch. XIII "The Draft", 23 Sep 1864): "in company with my brother Peter and Peter Hershiser, I was riding to New Haven" - drafted with P. M. Hershiser; he enlisted instead into the 64th OVI (matches log: 55th 24 Oct 1861-11 Dec 1862, then 64th 23 Sep 1864-18 Jun 1865). Peter M. Hershiser was his bunkmate in the 64th (Catharine Jane "Kate" Keesy's husband was a Hershiser per the log - probably the same family; UNCONFIRMED).

HOW THIS CHANGES THE LOG
1. Gives published exact BIRTH DATES for all ten Keesy siblings, including Margaret b. 13 Mar 1833 (log: "1833"), and John Keesy d. 18 Jan 1859 (log agrees) and mother Elizabeth d. Sept 1875 (log: 21 Sep 1875 agrees) - the book is a primary family source written by a son.
2. DISCREPANCY: the book says John (b. 1804) was born in Lancaster or Dauphin County, PA; the log has Moreland Twp, Lycoming Co.
3. Says "Henry's father was a Revolutionary soldier" -> Henry = Henricus Henry Giese Keesy (1762-1856) per log, so this points to his father Johann Conradt Giese/Gysi (1718-1802, York Co. PA) as the claimed Revolutionary soldier; that is a DAR/PA Archives lead (UNCONFIRMED; Johann Conradt would be 58 in 1776). Note log says Henricus himself (b. 1762) lived to 1856 in Huron Co. - the book's "Henry" who "was in the war of 1812" fits him.
