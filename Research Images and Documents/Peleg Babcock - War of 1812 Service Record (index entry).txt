Note: index-only entry, no page image available in this database (no Roll Box number assigned/no image
digitized). This is Peleg W. Babcock (1748-1834), already the subject of an SAR Membership Application
citing his Revolutionary War service; this separately documents a later War of 1812 militia service in
Vermont as a Private, 4th Regiment (Williams), at an age of roughly 64-67. Worth flagging as a fairly
advanced age for militia service, though not implausible for older men on militia muster rolls.
