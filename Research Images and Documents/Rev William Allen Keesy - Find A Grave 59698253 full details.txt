Rev William Allen Keesy (1843-1910)
Find A Grave Memorial ID: 59698253
https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/59698253/william-allen-keesy

BIRTH: 25 Jul 1843, Huron County, Ohio, USA
DEATH: 15 Apr 1910 (aged 66), Tiffin, Seneca County, Ohio, USA
BURIAL: Bethel Cemetery, Willard, Huron County, Ohio, USA
PLOT: Section A, Row 5, #6

Rev. William "Allen" Keesy was 5'6 1/2" tall with a fair complexion, blue eyes, and brown hair.

Allen served twice in the U.S. Army during the Civil War:
- 1st: 55th Ohio Infantry (24 Oct 1861 to 11 Dec 1862)
- 2nd: 64th Ohio Infantry (23 Sept 1864 to 18 June 1865)

Allen [1st] married Margaret Jane (Lane) Keesy ["Maggie"] (23 Oct 1845 - 24 Sept 1873) on 7 July 1868
in Wyandot County, Ohio.

Allen was [2nd] married to Harriet Augusta (Charles) Keesy ["Hattie"] (14 Sept 1856 - 1 May 1929) on
9 Feb 1875 by Rev. William Mathers of Oceola, Crawford County, Ohio.

The other known child of Allen and Hattie was Robert Ivan Keesy (born 23 June 1898), born in Bucyrus,
Crawford County, Ohio. Robert married Mabel (Kille) Keesy (30 Nov 1900 - 13 July 1982) on 20 May 1922
in Seneca County, Ohio. She was born in Freedom, Wood County, Ohio, to Freeborn Kille (1871-1933) and
Cora (VanAlst) Kille Taylor (1871-1955). Mabel died in Contra Costa, California.

Gravesite Details: White Bronze (Zinc) monument.
