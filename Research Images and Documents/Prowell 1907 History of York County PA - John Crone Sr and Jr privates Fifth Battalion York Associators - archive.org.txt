RELEVANCE (UNCONFIRMED -- name + county only): The Crone family book (F. L. Crone, 1916) says Robert's John Crone (b. 1789 at "Little York" = York PA; d. 1871 near Lucas, Richland Co., Ohio; m. Elizabeth Pence) was probably the son of Jacob Crone, who was himself "probably the son of John Crone of Windsor Township" (York Co.). A John Crone Sr. + John Crone Jr. served as privates in the York County Associators in the Revolution (1775-76). That is the kind of document that could, if the Windsor Township John Crone is the same man, make a Revolutionary patriot of Robert's 5x/6x-great-grandfather. It is NOT proven: only the name and county match.
