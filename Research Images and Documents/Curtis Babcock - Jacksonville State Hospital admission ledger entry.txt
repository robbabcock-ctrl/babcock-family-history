"B Surnames" table, row for Curtis Babcock:

Name: Babcock, Curtiss
Age: 36
Nativity: NY
Admitted: 26 Jan 1858
Discharged: 21 Jul 1858
Co. of Res.: Stephenson
Nearest Kin/Guardian/Notes: Brother of Mrs. Davis of that county

CONTEXT: This confirms and completes the timeline of Curtis Babcock's 1858 commitment as an "insane
pauper" (see the Freeport Journal, 7 Jan 1858 clipping saved elsewhere in this folder, and the 14 Jan
1858 county board resolution appointing a committee to take charge of him). He was transferred to
Jacksonville State Hospital on 26 Jan 1858 and discharged alive on 21 Jul 1858 (confirmed NOT on the
hospital's companion deceased-patients list) -- returning to Stephenson County, consistent with him
being alive and enumerated alone in the 3 Jul 1865 Illinois State Census. Age 36 in Jan 1858 implies
a birth year of approximately 1821-1822.
