ANALYSIS
- The research log has David Ringle Sr. (1801 Hempfield, Westmoreland Co. PA - 31 Mar 1881 Attica, Seneca Co. OH) and sons David Jr., Simon, Finley, William. This 1886 county history shows a David Ringle Sr./Jr. pair serving as Venice Twp trustees (1868-77), Finley Ringle platting an addition to Attica (1874), and an "S. A. Ringle" (Simon A.?) as Attica merchant/clerk - all matching names AND place. UNCONFIRMED as the same people (no bio sketch of David Sr. found in this book) but a strong consistency check; no contradictions.
- NEW detail not in the log: David Jr. = township trustee; Finley Ringle a landowner/platter in Attica 1874; S. A. Ringle = Attica storekeeper. Mrs. David Ringle was an original member of the Attica Methodist Protestant Church (1866) - the log has Catharine Craiglow as David Sr.'s 2nd wife (m. 1848).
