Leta E. Chapman, a 56-year Salinas resident, died Thursday at her 232 Loma Drive home. She was 83.

Mrs. Chapman was a homemaker and a native of Brandon, Iowa. She was a member of the Pentecostal Church of God and past president of the Senior Citizens for Social Justice.

Surviving are three sons, Claude of Pacific Grove, Dee Roy of Salinas and Paul of Morgan Hill; two daughters, Hazel Knowlton and Zelma Logan, both of Oregon; three sisters, Alma Barlow of Gonzales, Thora Jenkins of Woodland and Hala Mann of Colorado; four brothers, Dean Steckman of Oklahoma, Dan Steckman of Colorado, Roy Steckman of San Diego, and Alvin Steckman; 28 grandchildren, 45 great-grandchildren and one great-great-grandchild.

Mrs. Chapman was preceded in death by her husband, Arthur B. Chapman, who died in 1971; a son, Lee Chapman, who died in 1975; and a daughter, Ruth Marker, who died in 1981.

Funeral services will be Tuesday at 2 p.m. at the Pentecostal Church of God. The Rev. Morris Owens, pastor of the church, will officiate.

Burial will be in the Garden of Memories Cemetery.
