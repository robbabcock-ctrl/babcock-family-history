1) archive.org full-text search hit: "The Cemeteries of Noble County, Indiana, Volume IV: Allen, Green, Jefferson, Swan Townships" (archive.org id cemeteriesofnobl00unse). Index snippet: "RAWSON  ANSON G. RAWSON  FRANCES RAWSON  INFANT RAWSON  LIZZIE J. RAWSON". Consistent with burial at Allen Chapel Cemetery (Allen Twp, near Kendallville) per Crone 1936 (Switzer, Stuckey and Allied Families): Anson G. d. Sept 1, 1860 aged 63y 3m 27d; Frances d. April 24, 1867. "Lizzie J. Rawson" may be Silas and Barbara's daughter Elizabeth (died at age nine per Crone 1916). Volume not read in full.

2) "Six Generation Ancestor Tables, Bicentennial Project" (archive.org sixgenerationanc0003vari): "22 Anson G. Rawson, 1797-1860, NY; Noble Co., IN / 23 Francis Isbell" -- a lineage-society compiled table (compiler unidentified) that corroborates the pair.

3) New York State Archives, Series A3352 "Claim applications for service in the War of 1812" (1857-61), Name Index (https://www.archives.nysed.gov/research/name-index-by-series/A3352/R?page=1): Rawson, Anson - Claimant - accretion A3352-85 - Noble, Indiana - Claim 13543 - Box 53, Folder 9 - Microfilm roll 70. Neighbor: Isbell, Charles - claim 13541 - Noble, Indiana (Charles Isbell is Frances's brother per the log). No digitized image online; the index page offers an email request to archref@nysed.gov (researcher services). The claim form records regiment, company, commander, dates of service, land warrant number. NOTE: the log calls claim 13543 a "pension claim, certificate #13543"; A3352 is a New York STATE claim file, so a federal pension check (NARA M313 index / Fold3 / FamilySearch) is a separate question.

4) FTS snippet from "The Descendants of Robert Isbell in America" (Edna Warren Mason, 1944; archive.org descendantsofrob00maso, borrow-only): "born Salisbury, Conn., July 16, 1774, died Noble Co., Ind., February 1, 1851, probably in [..] there in home of her daughter, Mrs. Anson [Rawson]" -- this is evidently Mary (Chatfield) Isbell, mother of Frances. Log lists Joel Isbell d. 1865 and Mary Chatfield b. 16 Jul 1774 (matches). Needs a free archive.org account to borrow/read the page.
