ANALYSIS
- Confirms John Keesy (Geesy)'s Richmond Twp, Huron Co. residence from 1842 until death 1859 and shows him as a local officeholder (justice of the peace) and Whig/Republican - an addition to the log (which has only birth/death/marriage).
- Richmond Twp is where Rev. W. A. Keesy lived; source of 55th OVI Co. I recruits (Franklin, Co. I, 55th OVI) - the 55th OVI Company I roster would list W. A. Keesy (the log already has his service from another source).
