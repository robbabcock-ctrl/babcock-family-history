ANALYSIS
- Matches the log's Marietta Blackwell/Pfeffer Brand: d. Tues 1 Nov 1938 11 p.m. (log: death cert, St. Louis City Hospital, 11 pm); wife of Jacob Brand; maiden/adoptive name Pfeffer; children David Brand OF MONROVIA, CALIF. (= Bernadine's father David Brand of Monrovia, CA per her obituary), Marietta Rohlfing (nee Brand) (independently confirms the daughter's married name Rohlfing and shows the "Behnling" in earlier notes was a misreading), and Vern. Funeral Sat. 5 Nov 1938 (log: buried Columbia IL Nov 5).
- First newspaper confirmation that a 1938 Brand family notice names the grandchildren's father "David Brand of Monrovia" - useful to anchor the Bernadine/Monrovia link.
- The notice as printed calls her "nee Pfeffer" (adoptive surname), consistent with the log's note that obituaries used the adoptive name.
