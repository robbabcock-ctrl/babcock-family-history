!! POLLY STRONG IS NOT LISTED among Asher Strong's children by Dwight (nor in the "Strong Family History, update" copy). The log's statement that Polly Strong (1780-1825, wife of Rev. Daniel Rawson) was Asher's daughter therefore has NO support in Dwight 1871 and should be treated as UNCONFIRMED (it rests on another Ancestry member tree profile).

Also: Dwight vol. 1 (archive.org historyofdescend001dwig) p. ~19: "Jerijah b. Dec. 12, 1665, m. to Thankful Stebbins July 10, 1700 ... 4. Seth, m. to Lois Strong of Northampton" -> CONFIRMS the log's Jerijah/Thankful/Seth/Lois line. Index entry: "Seth Strong of New Marlborough, 1482-90".

Dwight says nothing of Revolutionary service for Seth (b. 1716) or Asher. The log's "Private in Major Leavenworth's Company, artillery, Connecticut militia" for Seth (1716-1792) is not corroborated: Seth lived in New Marlborough, Berkshire Co., Mass., not Connecticut. The Connecticut Adjutant-General's "Record of Service of Connecticut Men" (1889, archive.org waroftherevolution00recorich) lists a Seth Strong only as a pensioner (p. 637) and at p. 215; probably a different, younger Seth Strong. DAR claim needs checking.

Supporting: Cooperstown history "A Centennial Offering" (Cooperstown, 1886; archive.org centennialofferi00shaw_0) lists Asher Strong among purchasers of lots in the "Great Barrington Purchase" (Pierstown) near Cooperstown -> consistent with Dwight's "removed to Cooperstown".
