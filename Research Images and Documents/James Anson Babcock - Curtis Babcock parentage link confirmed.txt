IMPORTANT CONFIRMATION: This record directly confirms James Anson Babcock (later of James City
County, Virginia -- see the several Virginia Gazette clippings in this folder about "J.A. Babcock")
is the son of Curtis Babcock and Loranda Goodman -- the same Curtis Babcock researched extensively
in the "Curtis Babcock / Solomon Babcock line" section of this project (Curtis Babcock of Scott,
Cortland Co. NY -> Oneco, Stephenson Co. IL). This closes the gap between the Cortland NY/Illinois
Babcock trunk and James Anson Babcock's later life in Virginia. Mother's name "Loranda Goodman" was
not previously documented in the Research Log and should be added/cross-checked against Curtis
Babcock's other records.
