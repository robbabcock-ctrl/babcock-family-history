ANALYSIS (agent note, all UNCONFIRMED leads)
- Kottwitz: the log has Albertine/Albertina Kottwitz (1852-1928) as wife of Ferdinand H. Schlottach (1841-1916), with ThruLines parents Johann Kottwitz + Katharina Gutwein. A cluster of Kottwitz farmers/businessmen around Bland/Rosebud (A. F., Aug. F. b. Germany 1863 arr. 1864, H. C., Hermann) shows the Kottwitz immigration reached Gasconade Co. about 1864 - consistent with Albertine (b. 1852) arriving as a child with her family and with the Schlottachs/Kottwitz both living near Bland (Charles Schlottach also P.O. Bland in 1913). Candidate brothers/cousins of Albertine; check "Aug. F. Kottwitz"'s 1888 biography / the 1888 Goodspeed History of Gasconade County if it names Albertine.
- Sewell: James A. Sewell (b. 1859, Gasconade Co., Red Bird P.O., County Judge) is the right age, place and surname to be a brother of Pleasant W. Sewell (1854-1921), father of Clara Jane Sewell (1878-1971) per the log (Red Bird is also where Clara's daughter Ruth was born in 1915). Needs one source naming both as sons of the same Sewell parents.
