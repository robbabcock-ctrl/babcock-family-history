Marie Ellen "Mary" (Perry) Wilson (24 Feb 1895, Gordon, Sheridan Co. Nebraska - 15 Aug 1953,
Lusk, Niobrara Co. Wyoming)
Daughter of Bertha Stella Keown Perry + Allen W. Perry; granddaughter of Thomas Henderson Keown +
Phoebe Ellen McKinney. Named as "Mrs. Mary E. Wilson of Lusk, Wyoming" in her mother Bertha's 1940
obituary (see companion file).

=== Obituary excerpt (The Lusk Herald, 20 Aug 1953) ===

"Marie Wilson Dies in Lusk Saturday

Mrs. Marie E. Wilson, resident of this community since 1936, died at the Spencer Hospital Aug. 15
at 12:00 a.m. after being an invalid for 15 years, and funeral services were conducted Monday
afternoon from the chapel of the Peet Mortuary.

The concluding rites were conducted by the Rev. O. F. Shroyer, pastor of the Church of God, at
2:00 p.m. Mrs. A. F. DeCastro and Mrs. Emerson Bonner sang two duet selections, "Rock of Ages" and
"Nearer My God to Thee." Mrs. J. P. Watson was chapel organist."
(Find A Grave's excerpt is truncated with a "Read More" -- this is the portion captured; the full
article may have additional detail not retrieved this session.)

NOTE: This confirms she was an invalid for the last 15 years of her life (since ~1938) and had
lived in the Lusk, Wyoming area since 1936 -- consistent with her mother Bertha's 1940 obituary
already listing her there as "Mrs. Mary E. Wilson of Lusk, Wyoming."
