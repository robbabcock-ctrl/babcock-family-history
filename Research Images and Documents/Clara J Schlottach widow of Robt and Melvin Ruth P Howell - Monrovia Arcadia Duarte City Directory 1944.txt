ANALYSIS (agent note)
- Clara J. Schlottach, widow of Robt, = Clara Jane (Sewell) Schlottach (1878-1971), widow of Robert C. Schlottach (d. 1922). She was living in MONROVIA, CA by 1944, rooming at the address of Melvin (and Ruth P) Howell = daughter Ruth Pearl Schlottach (b. 1915) and husband Melvin E. Howell (log: m. Melvin E. Howell; Melvin shown as USA = in the Army in 1944).
- Monrovia is where her daughter Stella (Estella Schlottach Brand) and son-in-law David Brand were living (Bernadine's obituary: "David and Stella Brand of Monrovia, CA"). The 1944 directory has no David Brand entry in the text I searched (OCR-limited), so this does not itself place the Brands there in 1944; the Brands' Monrovia residence is supported by the 1938 Post-Dispatch notice naming "David Brand of Monrovia".
- Identifiers matching: surname + Clara J + widow of Robt + (Ruth P/Melvin Howell household). Treated as a good match (CONFIRMED as the same family only via those three; strongly consistent).
- Adds: Clara's residence 1944 (log has only 1971 death, no residence).
