NOTE: This confirms Elisabeth Regener Brand (first wife, per Find A Grave, 1838-1879) died before
1880 and Joseph remarried Margretha (maiden name Scheller -- see marriage record below) that same
year. Cathrina (age 6 in 1880, b. ~1874) is the "one daughter, all grown" mentioned in Joseph's
1903 obituary alongside sons Peter, William, and Jacob -- this had been an open question (the
obituary names the daughter's siblings but not her), now resolved: her name is Cathrina/Catherine
Brand. Since Cathrina and Jacob were both born years before Margretha's 1880 marriage, they (like
William and Peter) are children of Joseph's first wife Elisabeth, and Margretha was their
stepmother by the time of this census.
