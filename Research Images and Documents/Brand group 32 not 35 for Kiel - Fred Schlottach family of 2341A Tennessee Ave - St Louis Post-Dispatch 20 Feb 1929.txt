TRANSCRIPTION (OCR-cleaned; short item under the headline)
"ONLY 32 BRANDS ARE FOR KIEL. Three of Family's 35 Members Listed as Supporters Back Miller. The Kiel-for-Mayor organization, which has been vying with the Miller-for-Mayor group in counting the noses of families of the same political mind, counted three too many when it announced that the Brands, of Ohio avenue, and their relatives by birth and marriage, totaling 35 voters, would vote en bloc for Kiel. The Fred Schlottachs of 2341A Tennessee avenue, including [Fred] Schlottach, his wife, Lydia, and his mother, Mrs. Martha Schlottach, listed with the Brand group, today announced they were and always had been Miller supporters, thus reducing the Brand vote to 32."
