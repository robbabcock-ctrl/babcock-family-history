The Fresno Bee coverage of Jerry Wayne Looney's death, transcribed (no image saved -- see note below)

--- Tuesday, June 14, 1966, page 22 ---

KINGS SOLDIER JERRY LOONEY IS KILLED IN VIET
McClatchy Newspapers Service

HANFORD, Kings Co. -- The Defense Department has notified Mr. and Mrs. Lehman C. Looney, 11749
Excelsior Ave., of the death of their son, Pfc. Jerry Wayne Looney, 21, in Viet Nam.

Looney's name appeared on a casualty list released yesterday by the government [remainder of article
below this line was not fully captured -- see The Hanford Sentinel and San Francisco Examiner
coverage of the same casualty list, saved elsewhere in this folder, for the full list of names and
context].

--- Sunday, June 19, 1966, page 29 ---

[Headline not fully captured] ...HANFORD, Kings Co. -- Funeral services for Jerry Wayne Looney, who
was killed in action in Viet Nam last Saturday, will be held tomorrow at 2:30 p.m. at the Peo[ple's
Funeral Chapel, page continues...]
