TRANSCRIPTION (OCR-cleaned; short local item)
"Mrs. Johanna K. Pfeffer Dies. Drs. F. J., P. A. and C. A. Pfeffer, all of St. Louis, were informed this morning of the death yesterday in Columbia, Ill., of their mother, Mrs. Johanna K. Pfeffer, widow of John A. Pfeffer, former Mayor of [Columbia]. Mrs. Pfeffer was [about 70] years old [OCR: 'years'] and had been ill only a week before her death. Her funeral will be held Thursday at Columbia."

ANALYSIS (agent note)
- Confirms Johanna K. (Funk) Pfeffer's death on 15 Nov 1915 at Columbia, IL (log: d. 15 Nov 1915) and her widowhood of John A. Pfeffer (d. 1908).
- NEW: names THREE physician sons in St. Louis - Drs. F. J. (Francis Joseph), P. A. (Peter A.) and C. A. Pfeffer. The log names only Peter A. and Francis Joseph (+ Theodore J.). "C. A." is a third doctor son not previously recorded (a Pfeffer physician in St. Louis, perhaps Dr. Charles A. Pfeffer - UNCONFIRMED).
- NEW: calls John A. Pfeffer "former Mayor" (of Columbia) - the log has him as village trustee/school board; the village head in Columbia, IL was styled mayor after city incorporation, so this may be a detail worth checking in Columbia city records (UNCONFIRMED, the OCR is damaged).
- The article does not mention Marietta/Mrs. Jacob Brand.
