Herman Schultz obituary - The Arroyo Grande Valley Herald Recorder, 13 Jan 1950, page 2
"Herman Schultz, 61, Dies Suddenly At Orcutt Home"

Full text: Funeral services for Herman August Schultz, 61, of Orcutt, were held Thursday afternoon of
last week in the Dudley Chapel in Santa Maria. The Rev. J. Ross Linsenmayer of the Orcutt Community
Church officiated. Burial was in the Santa Maria cemetery. Schultz died suddenly at his home in Orcutt
on January 2. He had been a resident of Orcutt for the greater part of the last 30 years. He was born
December 15, 1888 in Appleton, Wisconsin, and lived in California for the last 40 years.
