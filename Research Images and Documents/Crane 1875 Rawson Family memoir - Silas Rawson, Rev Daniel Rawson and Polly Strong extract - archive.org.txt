#722 Silas Rawson (Nathaniel, Nathaniel, William, Edward), m. 1st Abigail Chapin, eight children. 2d wife Rebecca Fellows. Settled first in Conway, Mass.; after death of 1st wife changed residence many times. Died at Palmyra, N.Y., Aug. 26, 1819, aged 80 years.
#1648 Rev. Daniel Rawson (Silas, Nathaniel, Nathaniel, William, Edward), m. Polly Strong, Sept. 1, 1796. Removed to Indiana in 1819. He was a Baptist minister. Died Sept. 17, 1824. She d. Aug. 16 [1825].
Footnote (OCR, copied from the original by W. C. Rawson, a grandson of Daniel): ordination certificate -- STATE OF INDIANA, LAWRENCE COUNTY. Convened at the place of meeting of the Sugar Creek church on 21 July 1821 at the instance of the Baptist Church aforesaid, to set apart by solemn ordination Daniel Rawson to the Gospel ministry. Signed: Abraham Stark, William Pope, Rice McCoy, Elders.
