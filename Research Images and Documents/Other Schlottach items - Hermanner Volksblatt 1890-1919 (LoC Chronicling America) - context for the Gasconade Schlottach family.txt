NOTABLE ITEMS (which are for which Schlottach branch is NOT established; the paper names several unrelated Schlottach/Schlottag households in the Bem/Drake/Owensville/Bland area)
- 28 Dec 1917 p8 (list of draft registrants called up): "... Ferd. H. Schlottach, Owensville ..." -> a Ferd. H. Schlottach lived at Owensville in 1917, one year after Ferdinand (b. ~1841) died there; consistent with the 1937 St. Louis Ferd H. Schlottach (d. 28 Nov 1937) being his son. UNCONFIRMED.
- 26 Jul 1907 p8 (Bem column): "Paul Schlottach von St. Louis ist hier zu Besuch." (Paul Schlottach of St. Louis is visiting here [Bem].) -> the Paul named among Ferd H.'s siblings in 1937 lived in St. Louis by 1907 and visited Bem, the church-village of Ferdinand Schlottach's Evangelical congregation. UNCONFIRMED.

Deppe ..." visiting the old home at the holidays (Charles Schlottach and wife, visitors from elsewhere); 13 Apr 1906 p8: "Charles Schlottach travelled ... to Lorton, Nebraska ... will also visit Lincoln" (Charles Schlottach b. 1875 Gasconade per the 1913 atlas?) - UNCONFIRMED.
- 21 Jan 1916 p8: see separate file (Ferdinand Schlottach obituary).
- 28 Jan 1916 p5: "Frau Henry Schlottach, früher Frl. Barbara Jaide" now of Enid, Okla., underwent surgery; 18 Feb 1916: "der junge Willie Schlottach" ill; 31 Mar 1916: "Leo Schlottach's Söhnchen Harold" ill; 19 May 1916: "Wilhelm Schlottach's youngest son baptised Arthur Albert"; 24 Sep 1915 p1: "Frau Schlottach" was badly hurt - her arm near the elbow was caught in a manure-spreader/machine that son William and her husband were pushing (accident report; first name of the family not legible) -> other Schlottach households: Henry, Wm./Wilhelm (near Drake), Leo, Hermann (Wittenberg/Drake: "Hermann Schlottach" b. ~1859? sawmill owner), Fred/Tina/Alma (Hermann's children) - the Gasconade Schlottachs are numerous; these are likely cousins/uncles/brothers of Ferdinand but NO item here ties them to Ferdinand H.
- 3 Apr 1908, 12 Nov 1909 and others: Hermann Schlottach/Schlottag (Wittenberg, Drake area), "birthday surprise party" 1909.
- 24 Apr 1914 p8: wedding of Theodor Schulte and Frl. Alma Schlottach at the home of the bride's parents, Hermann Schlottach and wife (Rev. Specht, Evangelical Church, Owensville) - not Stella's sister Alma (b. 1901).
- 17 Oct 1919 p8 marriage licenses: Willi A. Schlottach, Owensville, 22 + Anna M. Glaser, Owensville 18.
