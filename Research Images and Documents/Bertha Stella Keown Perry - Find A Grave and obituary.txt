=== Obituary (undated clipping, evidently a Gordon, Nebraska newspaper, June 1940) ===

"MRS. ALLEN W. PERRY

Stella Bertha Keown was born in Concordia, Kansas, January 16, 1873, and passed away at the
General Hospital in Gordon, Nebraska June 8, 1940, aged 67 years, 4 months and 12 days. Although
she had been in ill health over a period of time, death came to her release as the culmination of
nine weeks of intense suffering.

In the year 1883, Miss Keown came with her parents to Hay Springs, Nebraska; and on November 26,
1892, she was united in marriage to Allen W. Perry at Gordon, Nebraska, the Rev. J. A. Scamahorn
performing the ceremony. To this union eleven children were born: eight sons and three daughters.

At an early age, Mrs. Perry united with the Methodist Episcopal Church, and for the past fifty
years she devoted her strength to farming and to the rearing of her children.

She leaves to mourn her departure six sons, Claude of Jacksonville, Oregon, Thomas E., Edward M.,
Lester J. and William of Gordon and Charles B. of Wheatland, Wyoming; her three daughters, Mrs.
Mary E. Wilson of Lusk, Wyoming, Mrs. Lula E. Eden and Mrs. Edna M. Hall of Gordon; two sisters,
Mrs. Ethel Ball of Orcutt, California, and Mrs. Edna M. Schultz of Santa Cruz, California; fourteen
grandchildren and five great grandchildren, besides a host of friends who through the years have
admired her for her many sterling qualities. She was preceded in death by her husband, and two
sons, Johnnie E. and Clare who died in infancy.

Funeral Services were conducted at the Gates Funeral Home Tuesday afternoon, June 11. The Rev. W.
B. Pardun, pastor of the First Methodist Church officiating and interment was made in the Gordon
cemetery."

KEY NEW DETAILS resolving other checklist items:
- Confirms the family's migration path: Missouri (1870 marriage) -> Nelson, Cloud Co. Kansas (1880
  census, misindexed "Cowan") -> Hay Springs, Nebraska (1883) -> and separately, some of the
  family (Thomas & Phoebe themselves, plus Ethel) continued on to Arroyo Grande, California by
  1896/97, while Bertha stayed in the Gordon, Nebraska area after her 1892 marriage.
- "Mrs. Mary E. Wilson of Lusk, Wyoming" is almost certainly the "Marie Ellen (Perry) Wilson,
  b.1895-d.1953" from the separate Wyoming death-records checklist item -- her married name and
  state (Wyoming) match exactly; "Mary E." is likely a variant/nickname rendering of "Marie Ellen."
- Sister "Mrs. Edna M. Schultz of Santa Cruz, California" corroborates Edna Matilda (Keown)
  Schultz (already saved elsewhere in this project via her 1916 Phoenix AZ marriage record and her
  husband Herman Schultz's obituary).
- Note: this obituary gives Thomas Henderson Keown and Phoebe's more precise life dates (1846-1933
  and 1850-1934) via the Find A Grave memorial data above, consistent with "d. 1933" already noted
  in the Research Log for Thomas.
