Full text: Cynthia was born on January 22, 1949, and died on Tuesday, December 3, 2002. She was the
second of five children. She was educated at Fresno State College graduating with a bachelor's degree
in Industrial Arts and a teaching credential. She worked as a pharmaceutical sales representative for
Merck and for Colgate-Hoyt for many years supporting herself and raising her son.

She was a gifted artist and a member of LAPAPA, Alliance for California Artists and worked on the
Pageant of Master's two years. She loved to travel in Europe, China and United States.

She is survived by her loving son, Scott Babcock of Tulare, CA; her parents, John E. and Marjorie
Rawson of Dinuba, CA; sisters, Donna Paulsen of Kingsburg, CA, and Jeanette Krone of Fresno; two
brothers, John A. [Rawson] of Orange, CA, and Robert Rawson of Kingsburg; and two grandchildren.
