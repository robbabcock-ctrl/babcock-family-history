PROMINENT COUNTY MAN DIES SUDDENLY
James A. Babcock, of Five Forks, Will be Buried This Afternoon at 3 O'clock.
(Williamsburg Bureau)

James A. Babcock, aged 83, prominent farmer and church worker of Five Forks, died Sunday afternoon
while visiting a son at Lee Hall. He will be buried this afternoon at 3 o'clock. Funeral services
will be held at the Five Forks Presbyterian church with the Rev. Harrup and the Rev. Ware, of Lee
Hall, officiating. He was an elder of the Five Forks church.

Mr. Babcock was born in New York state. After moving to Illinois, he came to Virginia in 1908.
Since that time he had been engaged in farming in the Five Forks section. He retired several years
ago.

He is survived by the widow, four sons, James, John, William, and Harvey; 30 grandchildren, 20
great-grandchildren, and several great-great-grandchildren.

Pallbearers this afternoon will be elders in the church, Jesse Dimmick, D. C. Renick, E. M. Slauson,
J. B. Vaiden, George Freeman, and John Hofmeyer.
