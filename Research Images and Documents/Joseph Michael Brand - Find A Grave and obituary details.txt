=== Civil War service note (from grave registration card, added by Mary) ===
"Joseph Brandt's grave registration card which was completed by Albert C. Metter as American
Legion Post 581 Grave Registrar, circa 1930, indicates he served in Company D, 1st Regiment,
United States Reserve Corps, Missouri Infantry."

1st Regiment United States Reserve Corps Infantry organized for three months at St. Louis, MO,
May 7, 1861. Attached to Lyon's Army of the West. Capture of Camp Jackson, St. Louis, May 10.
Provost duty in St. Louis and at the Arsenal until June 20. (Co "I" at Jacques Garden and
Urich's Cave entire service.) Moved to Jefferson City June 20-24. Duty at St. Louis until July
29. (Cos. "A," "F" and "L" moved to Rolla July 30, thence to Cuba August 4, and duty there until
August 10.) March to Bird's Point, MO, July 29-30, and duty there until August 13. Moved to St.
Louis, MO, August 13-14. Mustered out August 20, 1861.

Regiment lost during service: 1 officer and 3 enlisted men killed and mortally wounded, and 4
officers and 25 enlisted men by disease. Total 33.

=== Obituary: Waterloo Republican, Thursday, 16 Jul 1903 ===
Image saved: Joseph M Brandt obituary - Waterloo Republican 16 Jul 1903.jpg (added to Find A
Grave by user "Mary," 30 Apr 2023)

Full text transcription:

"Joseph Brand a well known carpenter and contractor, died on Saturday, July 11, at the home of
his [son] William Brand. The deceased was suffering from Bright's disease and the end came
fast. Mr. Brand was a man of affairs and undertaking in his prime. He built many large buildings
in Monroe Co., notably the fine Catholic church at Waterloo. He was a soldier of the civil war,
having served 3 years in the first Mo., regiment of infantry. For many years he was a member of
the town board here, and to his credit be it said he was one who always advocated progress and
improvement rather than retrogression. The deceased was a widower, but leaves three sons, Peter,
William and Jacob Brand, and one daughter, all grown. He was a member of Col. Ellsworth Post
G. A. R., the Columbia Turnverein and Waterloo Lodge Knights of Honor, which societies attended
his burial en masse. He was laid to rest at the Catholic cemetery on Monday afternoon, a large
concourse of friends following him to his final resting place. He was aged 73 years.

No False Claims."
