Content (from the SAR page):
- Name: Oliver BABCOCK Sr (SAR index label; this is the man the research log calls Oliver Babcock JR.)
- Birth: 16 Sep 1722, Westerly, Washington Co., RI.  Death: 24 Feb 1806, Leyden, Franklin Co., MA   (matches log dates exactly)
- State of service: RI.  Rank: Private.   
- Qualifying service: "Reformados Alarm Men, Westerly, RI, Captain Bliven"
- Spouse: Patience Pendleton.  Children listed: Peleg, Paul, Ezra, Luke, Oliver Jr.
- DAR #: A004277.  Burial: West Leyden Cemetery, Leyden MA (Find a Grave memorial 6824294).
- Source cited: "RI ST ARCH INDEX TO MIL & NAV RECS, 1774-1805", FHL film 934759 (an INDEX, not the muster roll itself).
- The page states it "cannot be used directly as proof of Qualifying Service or Lineage"; five SAR members already claim descent via Peleg, Paul, Luke and others.

WHY IT MATTERS: The research log (sessions 43-44, 2026-09-11) records a photo caption calling Oliver "Lieut." Both the SAR and DAR (A004277) indexes give his rank as PRIVATE in the Westerly alarm company (he was aged ~53-54 in 1776, so alarm-list/reformado service is plausible). "Lieut." appears to belong to a different Oliver Babcock (WeRelate lists a Lt. Oliver Babcock b. 28 Jun 1746 Hopkinton, probably Oliver's son; unverified).
