---- (1) Crone 1916, Second Generation, John Crone (No. 3) ----
"John Crone was born in Franklin County, Pa., in 1818 [OCR 1^1-] and died at Kendallville, Indiana, in 1898. He married Catherine Switzer who was born at Lucas, Ohio, in 1822 and died at Garrett, Indiana, in 190[?]. They removed to Kendallville, Indiana, the fall of 1849. They were married in 1839. Children: Daniel (15); Elizabeth (16); Barbara (17); Joseph (18); Mary Jane (19); John S. (20); William H. (21); Sarah Ann (22); Amy R. (23); Laura Irene (24)."
(NB the log gives Catharine b. 27 Feb 1821 Richland Co OH, d. 1907 -- close but not identical; the 1916 book's 1822 / "190?" digits are OCR-uncertain.)

---- (1) Crone 1916, First Generation, No. 1 John Crone ----
"John Crone was born [..] 1789 at York, Pa., then known as Little York. He died near Lucas, Ohio, in 1871. He was a son of Jacob Crone, [probably] ... who was a soldier in the Revolution. A Jacob Crone lived in York County in 1782 and was then a bachelor. He was [probably] the son of John Crone of Windsor Township. John Crone (1) married Elizabeth Pence who was born at York in 1794 and died near Lucas March 26, 1870. The marriage probably took place in 1812."
Pp. ~41-42: the author lists four Revolutionary soldiers named Crone from Pennsylvania Archives, 3rd series: Henry Crone (1st Pa. Regt, Continental Line); Noble Crone (Capt. Joseph Howell's Co., 2nd Pa.); Jacob Crone (private in Capt. Thomas Hinge's [OCR] Company, 6th Pa. Regiment under Lt. Col. Josiah Harmar, 1778); Thomas Crone (Capt. Miller's Co., 1st Pa. Continental Line, 1776). "The writer believes that this Jacob Crone was the father of John Crone born in 1789." (author's belief, NOT proven)
Immigrant possibilities listed: Henrich Cron arrived 7 Sept 1748 on ship Hampshire (Rotterdam/Falmouth) etc.

---- (2) Switzer, Stuckey and Allied Families (1936), No. 53 Barbara Crone ----
"Barbara Crone (Jan. 16, 1843 - August 1919) married Silas Rawson at Kendallville Dec. 24, 1861 [digits garbled]. Silas Rawson (April 4, 1837 - Oct. 27, 1908) was born in Medina County, Ohio. His father Anson G. Rawson died Sept. 1, 1860, aged 63 years, 3 months and 27 days. His mother Frances Rawson died April 24, 1867, aged 70 years, 2 months and 2[?] days. Both are buried at the Allen Chapel cemetery near Kendallville. According to the 1860 census both Anson G. and Frances Rawson were born in New York. In 1860 they lived in Jefferson Township, Noble County, Indiana. Silas and Barbara Rawson had: Elizabeth died at the age of nine; Clara (54); Annetta Frances (55); Herbert (56); William H. (57); Laura (58); George (59); Walter (60); Mabel (61)."
