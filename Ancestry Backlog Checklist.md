**How to use this:** Work one family group at a time, oldest generation first within each group (so parents/spouses exist before you add their children). Status tags:
- **[CONFIRMED]** = well-sourced (primary record, published genealogy, or multiple independent corroborating sources) — safe to enter as a solid fact.
- **[UNCONFIRMED]** = single thin/compiled-tree source, or an explicitly flagged open question — enter as a research note / tentative fact, not an assertion.
- **[DECISION]** = a labeling/naming choice Robert needs to make, not a missing fact.
- **[OPEN QUESTION]** = no specific fact to enter yet, just a still-unresolved research thread (listed for awareness, not action).
- Where the log corrected itself, only the **final/corrected** version is listed as an entry item; the superseded version is noted in a parenthetical "(originally X, corrected to Y)" so nothing is silently lost.
