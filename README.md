# Babcock, Chapman, Blackwell & Brand Family History

This repository is the permanent, shareable archive for Robert Babcock's family history
research (Ancestry.com tree "Robert Babcock Family Tree", tree ID 101867177). It exists so
that everything gathered and learned across this research project survives independently of
any one person's Ancestry.com login, browser, or computer — anyone given access to this repo
can read, search, and reuse everything in it.

## What's here

- **`Research Log - Master Notes.md`** — the master, chronological research journal. This is
  the single most complete record of everything learned: every person researched, every source
  checked, every conclusion reached (and every open question still unresolved), plus the
  research techniques and pitfalls discovered along the way. Start here if you want the full
  story of the project.

- **Narrative write-ups (`.docx` / `.pdf`)** — polished, readable accounts pulled together from
  the research log for specific people or family lines:
  - *The Chapman Family Album - Findings.docx* — the Chapman/Kimball/Babcock photo collection review
  - *Ann Blackwell - Life and Headstone Reference.docx* — her life story and the facts needed for a headstone
  - *From Westerly - A Documented History of the Babcock Family.docx*
  - *The Girl from the Poorhouse (Fourth Edition).pdf* — Mary/Marietta Blackwell
  - Records-request letters sent to Illinois State Archives and the Rome, NY City Clerk

- **`Research Images and Documents/`** — photos of actual records, gravestones, obituaries,
  poorhouse ledgers, census pages, and other source images found and confirmed during research,
  plus a few shorter working papers, that aren't already embedded in one of the narrative docs above.

- **`Source Texts (digitized books and records)/`** — full text/scans of published genealogies
  and record volumes that were searched to confirm specific facts (e.g. the 1903 *Babcock
  Genealogy*, Bagg's *Pioneers of Utica*, Rhode Island colonial records, Cortland Co. records).
  These are public-domain sources, kept here so a specific citation can always be re-checked
  without depending on a library site staying up or a search index turning up the same result twice.

- **`Aunt Sandi New Photos 2026-09-08/`** and **`Bernie Scrapbook Photos/`** — original family
  photos contributed by family members, plus a `_GENUINELY_NEW` subfolder isolating the ones
  that hadn't been seen/catalogued before.

- **Family tree charts** (`james-to-sandi*`, `solomon-to-sandi*`) — org-chart-style visual
  family trees, as both the rendered PNG and the HTML/SVG source used to generate it.

## What's *not* here yet

A handful of very large bulk digitized-record scans (three files, roughly 500MB combined —
a 1930 census index and two multi-hundred-page court/session record sets) were left out because
a single file over 100MB can't be pushed to GitHub without extra tooling (Git LFS), and these
three are public-domain volumes that can be re-fetched from their original source (HathiTrust /
Internet Archive) if ever needed again — they aren't unique family material. They still exist on
Robert's own machine. Ask Robert (or Claude) if a specific citation from one of them needs to be
pulled back out and added here.

## Sourcing convention

Throughout the research log and the narrative documents, facts are marked as either
**CONFIRMED** (backed by a primary source — a census, vital record, or direct family
confirmation) or **UNCONFIRMED** (family memory, a single uncorroborated source, or a
reasonable hypothesis that still needs checking). Please preserve that distinction if you add
to this archive — don't let a guess quietly become a "fact" in the next edit.
